GraphRAG reasoning

Multi-hop traversal over Neo4j + Qdrant — answers come from the fused graph, not guesswork.

Zero-trust citations

Every claim carries a Neo4j node ID, FIR line or CCTV timestamp. Unverified claims are blocked before they reach the officer.

Human-in-the-loop

Merges and edges under 85% confidence land in a quarantine queue — an officer approves before anything touches the master graph.

What-If overlays

"Assume this number belongs to Suspect X." Hypotheticals run on ephemeral graph branches — the master DB is never touched.

Cross-lingual NER

Hindi, regional scripts and degraded scans resolve into one unified English entity graph — aliases included.

Immutable audit trail

Every agent action is hash-chained. Tamper with one entry and the whole chain screams — built for court admissibility.
